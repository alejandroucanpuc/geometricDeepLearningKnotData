\section{Introduction}

We are interested on explain the applications of geometric deep learning techniques to the analysis and classification of knotted structures. We aim to leverage the unique properties of knots and their representations to improve the performance of deep learning models in this domain. We take inspiration on the work of J. Lennart. The main intention of this work is to complete the results and background of Lennart's work.
